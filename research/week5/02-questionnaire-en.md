# From timetable to classroom — questionnaire v0.2

Status: pilot-ready draft revised around timetable-to-classroom journeys. Do not distribute as final until the team fills the bracketed fields, checks course research requirements and completes a pilot. Estimated time: 8–10 minutes; verify during pilot. Q19–Q22 are placed before Q16 deliberately; question IDs are retained for version tracking.

## Participant information

We are [team/course name], conducting a student design project about planning and finding the way to classes at the University of Birmingham Edgbaston campus, from checking a timetable to reaching a classroom. We want to understand current experiences, including journeys that work well. You do not need to use any particular transport mode or support a new app.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip non-screening questions or stop before submitting. Please do not provide names, exact home addresses, student numbers or identifying details in written answers. We will use responses for [course assessment purpose/audience]. Data will be accessible to [team and specified assessors], stored in [approved storage], and deleted on [date]. Contact: [university email].

We will not request identifying information. Configure the survey platform not to collect names or emails, and check its metadata settings before describing the survey as anonymous. If submissions have no identifier, we may be unable to identify and remove an individual response after submission. Explain any actual withdrawal procedure here: [procedure]. Follow the course's approval/consent process before recruitment; this draft is not ethics approval.

### Q1. Eligibility and consent [required, one answer]

- I am aged 18 or over, currently study at the University of Birmingham, have travelled to the Edgbaston campus in the past four weeks, have read the information above and agree to participate.
- I do not meet these conditions or do not agree. → End survey without collecting substantive answers.

## Experiences in the past four weeks

Q11. How often did you experience each of the following? [matrix; Never / Once / 2–3 times / 4 or more times / Not applicable / Cannot remember]
- Uncertain when to leave to arrive on time
- Bus or train delays, cancellations or a missed service
- Uncertain which campus entrance to use
- Difficulty finding the intended building or its entrance
- Different tools showing information that was difficult to reconcile
- Weather affecting your journey

Q12. If any journey difficulty affected you, what happened? [select all]
- Arrived later than intended / Left much earlier than necessary / Changed transport mode or route / Spent more than intended / Felt stressed or uncertain / Decided not to make the trip / No noticeable impact / Other [text] / Prefer not to say
Use “No noticeable impact” and “Prefer not to say” as exclusive options. Show only if Q11 contains an experience, otherwise skip to Q13.

Q13. What, if anything, makes it difficult for you to use walking/wheeling, cycling or public transport for journeys to campus? [optional text]
You may say “nothing” or explain why these modes are unsuitable. Please do not disclose medical information or identifying details.

Q14. What changes, if any, would make you consider a lower-emission travel option more often? [choose up to three; randomise substantive options]
- Lower cost / More reliable services / Shorter total journey / Safer routes / Better accessibility / Better information about routes and connections / Less crowded services / Secure bike storage / No change needed, I already use these options / I would not consider changing at present / Other [text] / Not sure
Configure “No change needed, I already use these options”, “I would not consider changing at present” and “Not sure” as exclusive: choosing one clears all other selections. Test whether participants find this restriction accurate during pilot. Do not infer actual behavioural change from these intentions.

## Connecting timetable, travel and classroom information

Q19. Think about the most recent time you travelled to a class on campus. Where did you find its date, start time, building and room information? [select all]
- University timetable or portal / Calendar / Learning platform or course page / Email or message / Personal notes / Asked someone / Already knew / Other [text] / Cannot remember [exclusive]

Q20. During planning or travelling to that class, did you switch between different apps, websites or other information sources? [one]
- No / Yes, once / Yes, 2–3 times / Yes, 4 or more times / Cannot remember
Do not count routine switching unrelated to the journey.

Q21. What happened when using those sources for that journey? [select all; allow skip]
- Found what I needed without difficulty / Had to re-enter or search for the same information / Found inconsistent or unclear information / Had difficulty connecting the transport stop or gate to the building / Had difficulty locating the floor or room / Switching was helpful and suited my needs / Other [text] / Not applicable [exclusive] / Cannot remember [exclusive]
Show if Q20 reports switching; otherwise skip to Q22. Do not treat switching itself as a problem.

Q22. Once you reached the building, how did you find the classroom? [select all]
- Already knew / Building signs or room numbers / Floor plan or university map / Course instructions or timetable details / Asked someone / Could not find the room / Other [text] / Not applicable [exclusive] / Cannot remember [exclusive]
Optional follow-up: What, if anything, was unclear? Do not enter personal timetable details or identifying information.

Q16. How useful, if at all, would each type of information be for your own journeys? [matrix; Not at all useful / Slightly useful / Moderately useful / Very useful / Extremely useful / Not applicable]
- Door-to-building journey including the final campus walk
- A suggested departure time with the reason for any extra time
- Clear distinction between scheduled times and live predictions
- Building entrance and campus gate information
- Weather forecast for the expected journey period
- Comparison of cost, duration and estimated environmental impact
- A clear message when data is unavailable or out of date
- A single view linking class time, building, room and the stages of a journey
Randomise row order if the platform supports it. These are concept probes, not evidence of demand for an app.

## Form-builder configuration checklist

- Disable automatic name/email collection; verify organisation sign-in settings and who can access responses.
- Only Q1 must be answered. Handle missing values separately from Not applicable and Cannot remember.
- Q1 decline/ineligible → end. Q12 conditional logic tested. All “exclusive” selections validated.
- Q20 switching → Q21; other responses → Q22. Pilot Q19–Q22 terminology and whether participants count unrelated switching.
- Use short pages and responsive matrices; if mobile matrices are difficult, split Q11/Q16 into individual items.
- Do not present GreenGo screenshots before behavioural questions. Avoid telling participants that a sustainable travel assistant is the desired solution.
- Avoid incentives or collection methods not agreed by the team/course. Use approved storage and fill all information placeholders before distribution.
